Features

Everything you need to take notes without the friction.

Built for everyday notes: quick to open, easy to organise, and not dependent on an internet connection.

Notes & checklists

Write free-form notes or turn them into checklists. Changes are saved automatically, so there is no save button to forget.

Search titles, note bodies, checklist items, and label names. Results stay instant even when your notes pile up.

Privacy

Privacy is not an add-on.

Catatan Fleksibel has no app server, no analytics, and no ads. Your main data lives in a local database on your device.

  • No account required for core features.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • Access tokens are not written to the app database.
  • Sync runs only after you grant permission.

Read the privacy policy

The only scope requested is drive.file

The app cannot see your entire Drive. It can only access files it created, or files you open with this app.

On Android, tokens are managed by Google Sign-In. On desktop, tokens will be stored in the operating system's secure storage, not in the notes database.

Sync

One spreadsheet, owned by you.

If you enable sync, Catatan Fleksibel creates or finds a spreadsheet called Catatan Fleksibel - Data in your Google Drive. There is no intermediary server.

How it works

  1. Connect Google. Choose an account and approve the drive.file scope.
  2. Take notes as usual. Data is always saved locally first.
  3. Sync. The app pushes changes, pulls changes from other devices, and merges them.

Learn how Google data is processed

What gets synced

  • Notes and checklist items
  • Labels and note–label links
  • Reminders and their completion status
  • Ordering and change-tracking metadata

Note: sync is not a backup service. The spreadsheet belongs to you, so you can download or copy it at any time. Conflicting versions are not silently discarded; the app saves them as [Konflik] notes.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is my data sent to your servers?

No. Catatan Fleksibel does not operate an app server. Your data is stored on your device. If you enable sync, data is copied to your own Google Drive spreadsheet through the Google API.

Do I need a Google account?

No, not for the core features. A Google account is only needed if you want to use Google Sheets sync.

What can the app see in my Drive?

The app requests the drive.file scope, so it can only access files it created or files you open with the app. It cannot read your entire Drive.

Is there any analytics or advertising?

There is no usage analytics, no advertising, and no selling of data. This website also uses no cookies or tracking scripts.

How do I delete my data?

Delete notes in the app, empty Trash, and disconnect your Google account if you use sync. The spreadsheet remains in Drive until you delete it yourself. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app also removes the local database.

How do I report a bug or send feedback?

Open GitHub Issues. Please do not include private note content, tokens, or email addresses in a public issue.
